Arrow Escape – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fluxy Labs ("we", "us") handles information when you play Arrow Escape (package com.arrows.fluxylabs), a casual puzzle game for Android. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive your personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

To save your game, the app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Level progress, stars, coins, power-ups, streaks, achievements and unlocked cosmetics
  • Settings such as sound, music, vibration and language

This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. Advertising

Arrow Escape is free and supported by ads. We use these third-party ad networks:

  • Google AdMob – Privacy Policy, How Google uses data in ads
  • Unity Ads – Privacy Policy

These providers may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system version, language and network type
  • Ad interactions (for example, ad views, clicks and rewarded ad completions)

They use this information to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalize ads. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies linked above.

Your choices: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

4. Google Play services

The app uses Google Play's in-app update feature to tell you when a new version is available. This is handled by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state – to load ads and check for updates
  • Vibration – for haptic feedback during gameplay (can be turned off in Settings)

6. Children's privacy

Arrow Escape is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ad networks used in this app, please contact us and we will help you with the request.

7. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device and is kept until you delete it. Data collected by our ad partners is kept according to their own retention policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, such requests about advertising data should be sent to Google or Unity through the links above. You can also contact us and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: labsfluxy@gmail.com

لعبة Arrow Escape مجانية ومدعومة بالإعلانات، ونستخدم شبكات الإعلانات التالية:

قد تجمع هذه الجهات تلقائيًا معلومات مثل:

  • عنوان IP والموقع التقريبي المستنتج منه
  • طراز الجهاز وإصدار نظام التشغيل واللغة ونوع الشبكة

خياراتك: يمكنك إعادة تعيين المعرّف الإعلاني أو إيقاف الإعلانات المخصّصة من إعدادات جهازك (الإعدادات ← Google ← الإعلانات أو الإعدادات ← الخصوصية ← الإعلانات حسب جهازك). إذا كنت في المنطقة الاقتصادية الأوروبية أو المملكة المتحدة أو سويسرا، فقد يُطلب منك الموافقة قبل عرض الإعلانات المخصّصة.
